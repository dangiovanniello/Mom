# With You

A prototype website for a Catholic, pro-life initiative that supports pregnant and parenting mothers.

> Prototype only. All data is sample data held in the page's memory. Nothing a user enters is sent or saved, and it resets on reload.

## Our Catholic Mission

With You is a Catholic, pro-life initiative. We believe human life has dignity from conception, and that a mother and her child are never in competition for our care.

We also know why many women feel they have no real choice: rent that is due, a job that won't hold, a partner who left, a family that won't help. Our answer to those pressures is concrete. We help pay bills, find diapers, drive to appointments, fill out forms, and keep showing up after the baby is born.

### What that means in practice

- **Care for both lives.** The Church's teaching on the dignity of the child comes with the same teaching on the mother's life, health, and future. Caring for one never means forgetting the other.
- **Help that lasts.** A child needs a home, food, and care for years, and a mother needs a community for just as long. Our support continues after birth, when the casseroles usually stop.
- **Action over argument.** We believe the conviction that every life matters means little unless it shows up as rent paid, diapers delivered, and a hand to hold.
- **No shame, no pressure.** We don't shame, pressure, or argue. If you have had an abortion, you are as welcome as anyone, and we can point you to ministries that offer care after abortion.

### Faith is never a condition of help

You don't need to be Catholic to receive help. No one is asked to pray, attend anything, or share their story. Spiritual support and prayer are available for those who want them. For those who don't, nothing changes.

### What we promise

- Honesty about what we can and can't do.
- Help that never depends on faith.
- Your story stays yours. Donors never see it by default.
- Support for you after birth, not only before.

## What the Site Includes

- Intake flow that matches a visitor to resources by state and need
- Resource directory with search, filters, detail pages, and a way to report outdated listings
- Assistance request form with reference numbers and status tracking
- Messaging with real volunteers (never an AI), with a clearly labeled automated acknowledgment
- Role-based views: visitor, mother, volunteer, case coordinator, administrator
- Internal notes kept separate from messages, plus an access log
- Learn section that keeps Catholic teaching separate from health information, with sources shown
- Test donation flow through a stand-in checkout
- Quick exit button and a plain statement that messaging is not an emergency service

## Running It

It is a single file. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser. An internet connection is needed to load the Newsreader and Figtree fonts from Google Fonts. Without it, the page falls back to Georgia and system fonts.

## Before Going Live

This prototype leaves several things blank on purpose:

- Team hours, response times, tax status, and contact details
- Verified local listings (current local entries are fictional samples)
- Theological review of Catholic teaching and clinician review of health content
- Server with secure sign-in, database, and a hosted payment provider
- Privacy, retention, safeguarding, and volunteer screening policies
- Licensed photography (the current illustration is a stand-in)

## Emergency Notice

Messaging on this site is not an emergency service. If you or your child are in danger, call 911. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988.
